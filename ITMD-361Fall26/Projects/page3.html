Dream Career: Software Engineer

What is Software Engineering?

Software Engineering is the disciplined and systematic process of designing, tedting, and maintaing computer software using engineering principles

What is the difference between Computer Programing and Software Engineering?

Computer programming focuses primarily on writing code and software engineering looks at the big picture such as ensuring that digital programs are reliable, efficient, scalable, and built on time and within budget.

Many people believe a career in computer science isn't feasible through the rise of AI, however others believe AI serves as a tool for programmers rather than a threat to their career.
